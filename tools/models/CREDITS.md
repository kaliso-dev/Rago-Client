# Cosmetic model credits

`tools/models.py` converts these models into `assets/rago/models/cosmetics` and takes their
textures into `assets/rago/textures/cosmetics`. They were changed for Rago: moved into Minecraft's
model space, split into groups, hidden faces removed, the cap recoloured, and the wings tinted in game. The neon colours of the cap are made in game
by `NeonColors`.

| Minecraft Hats Mini-pack 3 | VoxelBear | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/minecraft-hats-mini-pack-3-e37b079ead7b42438e919eb88a8da7ee | [CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | `wizard_hat`, `wizard_hat.png` |

The Rago cap and beanie, and the dragon wings, are Rago's own (`tools/cosmetics.py`).
