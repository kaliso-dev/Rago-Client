# Cosmetic model credits

`tools/models.py` converts these models into `assets/rago/models/cosmetics` and takes their
textures into `assets/rago/textures/cosmetics`. They were changed for Rago: moved into Minecraft's
model space, split into groups, hidden faces removed, and the wings tinted in game.
