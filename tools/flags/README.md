# Flags

The national flags the flag capes are drawn from (`tools/flag_capes.py`), downloaded unchanged from
Wikimedia Commons, where each is in the public domain. The SHA-1 is Commons' own for the file.

The flags of the United States and of the United Kingdom are drawn again by `tools/flag_capes.py` from
their specifications (Executive Order 10834, the Union Flag's construction sheet), at the cape's
proportions; their SVGs here serve as references.
